## 2024-05-18 - ChatBot Screen Reader Accessibility\n**Learning:** Interactive floating action buttons (like the ChatBot toggle) must have dynamically updating `aria-label` and `aria-expanded` attributes to properly communicate their current state (open vs. closed) to screen readers. Relying solely on visual changes (like an icon swapping from Bot to X) is insufficient.\n**Action:** Applied dynamic `aria-label`, `title`, and `aria-expanded` to the ChatBot toggle, and added explicit `aria-label` to the internal Close and Send buttons. Also added keyboard `focus-visible` styles to ensure these interactive elements are clear for keyboard users.
